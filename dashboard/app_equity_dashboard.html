About this dashboard

Results come from equity_gaps_all_levels_long.csv, produced by app_equity_gaps.R from the OfS individualised student data. The population is full-time, UK-domiciled first degree students registered at Middlesex. Only the four academic faculties are shown.

Reading the trend line

Equity gap

The comparison group's rate minus the target group's rate, in percentage points. A positive gap means the target group does worse. BTEC entrants are compared with A-level entrants for continuation, completion and attainment, and with all other qualifications for progression.

Most recent year or 4-year aggregate

Most recent year shows the latest single year for each measure. 4-year aggregate combines the latest four years into one figure, as the OfS does. The aggregate is steadier where student numbers are small, so department and programme views open on it. The baseline is always the 4-year aggregate of the four baseline years.

Which milestone?

Results are compared with the 2026-27 milestone. The APP baselines use the data available when the plan was written, and each later milestone applies to the data available in that year. This file is the 2026 OfS release, which is the data the University holds during 2026-27.

Status

  • Target met: the gap is at or below the 2028-29 target.
  • Significantly ahead of milestone: the gap is below the 2026-27 milestone by more than its margin of error.
  • On track: the gap is at or below the 2026-27 milestone, but within the margin of error.
  • Behind milestone: the gap is above the 2026-27 milestone, but within the margin of error.
  • Significantly behind milestone: the gap is above the 2026-27 milestone by more than its margin of error.
  • Small numbers: either group has fewer than 10 students.

Is the gap to the milestone significant?

This asks whether the distance between an area's gap and the 2026-27 milestone is bigger than chance would produce, given how many students are involved.

Each gap has a 95% margin of error, worked out from the size of each group and its rate. If the gap minus its margin is still above the milestone, the area is significantly behind. If the gap plus its margin is still below the milestone, it is significantly ahead. Otherwise the difference from the milestone is not significant.

The status combines both questions: which side of the milestone the gap falls, and whether the difference is larger than the margin of error. The line under each status shows the distance from the milestone and the margin, so the judgement can be checked.

Tableau calculation: 1.96 * SQRT(pT*(1-pT)/nT + pC*(1-pC)/nC) * 100, where p is each group's rate and n its headcount.

Other equity gaps

This page reads equity_gaps_scan.csv and compares every demographic group in the OfS file with its comparison group, at each lifecycle stage. It looks beyond the APP targets, so new or growing gaps can be researched before the next plan is written.

  • Monitor closely: a significant gap of 10 pp or more over the latest four years.
  • Monitor: a significant gap of 5 to 10 pp.
  • Small but significant gap: a significant gap of under 5 pp.
  • No significant gap: within the margin of error.
  • Too few students: fewer than 20 students in either group over the four years.

These bands are set at the top of section 10 of the R script, and the dashboard applies the same bands to whichever scan file is loaded. The OfS file does not include care experience, estrangement, caring responsibilities, commuting, parental higher education, refugee status or Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities, so those groups are not covered.

Student numbers

Tables show the number of students in the target group and in the comparison group separately. For example, 27 BTEC and 16 A-level entrants.
