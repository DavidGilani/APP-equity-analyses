Funds and funnel stages (optional)

Set up a fund to see a funnel: the population, the students eligible for the fund, those who applied and those who received it, broken down by each demographic. Tick the cohort columns that mark who applied and who received the fund. Ticking several columns combines them, for example to group small donor funds into one. Eligibility filters work like the population filters above, and replace them for the same column (for example a different academic year).

The population filters and funds are remembered in this browser for next time. Save setup keeps them in a small file that holds column names and filter choices only, no student data.
